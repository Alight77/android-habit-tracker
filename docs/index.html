HabitTracker Privacy Policy

Developer: Alight77

Effective date: October 20, 2026

English 简体中文

1. Scope

This policy applies to the HabitTracker Android app. It explains how the app handles the habits and check-in records you create, and how Android system backup may affect them.

2. Data the app handles

When you create or edit a habit, the app saves the name you enter, the weekly goal you set, and the icon you choose. It also records when the habit was created. When you check in, the app saves the date and completion status. It uses these records to show your status today, your history, and statistics. Icon suggestions based on a habit's name are generated on your device.

For compatibility with existing data, the database still has a description field. The current add and edit screens do not provide a way to enter a description. If existing data includes a description, it remains stored with the corresponding habit.

3. Storage and transfer of data

Habits and check-in records are stored in an app-private database on your device, provided by Android. HabitTracker currently has no account or sign-in feature, no developer-operated cloud sync service, and no app feature that syncs these records to a developer server. Android system backup and device transfer may handle the database separately, as explained below.

4. Android system backup and restore

HabitTracker allows Android to include its app database in cloud backup and device transfer. If your device uses Google's Android cloud backup, you have enabled backup, and the system's conditions are met, the database may be included in a system backup associated with your Google Account. Some devices may also transfer the database through Android or device-maker transfer tools. System backup and device transfer do not mean that HabitTracker uploads your records to a developer server.

Whether a backup or transfer takes place, when it happens, and whether a restore succeeds depend on your Android version, device, system settings, and the applicable service. The app cannot guarantee that backup or restore will succeed or that a backup will immediately include your latest changes.

If the system retains an older backup, habits and check-in records deleted from the current device may reappear after a restore. Deleting a habit in HabitTracker cannot remove an existing system backup.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete the relevant habit, clear the app's data through Android settings, or uninstall the app. To delete a habit, use the more options menu on its habit card and review the confirmation message. Confirming deletion removes that habit and its related check-in records from the current app database. This action cannot be undone within the app.

Clearing local app data or uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete an older system backup. If that backup is restored later, old records may reappear. HabitTracker currently has no account deletion feature, no in-app button to erase all data at once, and no feature to remotely delete system backups.

6. Data security and external services

Local records use Android's app-private storage. Android and the applicable services handle cloud backup and device transfer. Their availability and protections depend on the device and system conditions. We do not promise that every backup uses the same form of encryption.

If you open this policy from the app, your browser requests the page from the website that hosts it. The hosting service may receive connection information needed to serve the page. This page's code contains no ads, analytics scripts, or other automatically loaded remote resources. It does not actively set cookies or use browser local storage. How the page host handles visits depends on the hosting service. Visiting the webpage is separate from storing habit records in the app.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or how HabitTracker handles data, contact alightx77@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, we will review and update this policy. The updated text and its effective date will be shown on the policy page.

HabitTracker 隐私政策

开发者：Alight77

生效日期：2026年10月20日

1. 适用范围

本政策适用于 HabitTracker Android 应用，说明应用如何处理您创建的习惯和打卡记录，以及 Android 系统备份可能产生的影响。

2. 应用处理哪些数据

创建或编辑习惯时，应用保存您输入的习惯名称、设置的每周目标和选择的图标。应用还会记录习惯创建时间。您打卡时，应用保存对应日期及完成状态，并用这些记录展示今日状态、历史记录和统计结果。根据习惯名称推荐图标的计算在设备上完成。

为兼容已有数据，数据库仍保留“描述”字段；当前新增和编辑界面不提供填写描述的入口。如果已有数据包含描述，它会随对应习惯保存在数据库中。

3. 数据存储与对外传输

习惯和打卡记录保存在设备上由 Android 提供的应用私有数据库中。HabitTracker 当前不提供账号、登录或由开发者运营的云同步服务，也没有将这些记录同步至开发者服务器的业务功能。Android 系统备份与设备迁移可能另行处理该数据库，具体见下一节。

4. Android 系统备份与恢复

HabitTracker 允许 Android 系统将应用数据库纳入云备份和设备迁移。如果您的设备使用 Google 提供的 Android 云备份，且您已开启备份并满足系统条件，数据库可能进入与您的 Google 账号关联的系统备份。部分设备也可能通过 Android 或设备厂商提供的设备间迁移流程传输数据库。系统云备份和设备迁移均不等同于 HabitTracker 向开发者服务器上传记录。

是否实际备份或迁移、何时执行以及能否恢复，取决于 Android 版本、设备、您的系统设置和相应服务条件。应用不能保证备份或恢复一定成功，也不能保证备份立即反映您刚做的修改。

如果系统保留了较早的备份，恢复时，已从当前设备删除的习惯及打卡记录仍可能重新出现。HabitTracker 不能通过应用内删除操作清除已有的系统备份。

5. 数据保留与删除

本机数据会保留到您删除相应习惯，或通过 Android 系统清除应用数据、卸载应用。您可以在习惯卡片的更多操作中选择“删除”，查看确认提示后删除该习惯。确认删除会从当前应用数据库移除该习惯及其关联的打卡记录；此操作不能在应用内撤销。

清除本机数据或卸载应用不代表较早的系统备份也已删除。若以后恢复该备份，旧记录可能重新出现。HabitTracker 当前没有账号删除、应用内一键清空全部数据或远程删除系统备份的功能。

6. 数据安全与外部服务

本机记录使用 Android 的应用私有存储。云备份和设备迁移由 Android 系统及相应服务处理，其可用性和保护方式取决于设备与系统条件；我们不承诺所有备份都采用同一种加密方式。

如果您通过应用内链接在浏览器中打开本政策，浏览器会向承载本页面的网站请求内容。网页托管服务可能接收提供页面所需的网络连接信息。本页面代码不包含广告、分析脚本或自动加载的其他远程资源，不主动设置 Cookie，也不使用浏览器本地存储。网页托管服务处理访问请求的方式取决于其实际服务。网页访问与应用内保存习惯记录是不同的处理过程。

7. 联系方式

如对本政策或 HabitTracker 的数据处理有疑问，请联系：alightx77@gmail.com。

8. 政策更新

如应用的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会审查并更新本政策。更新后的内容和生效日期将在政策页面展示。
